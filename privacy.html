Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Information stored on your device

Cube Buddy saves your solve times and related timer history in your browser's local storage so the progress display can work between visits. This information stays in that browser on that device; Cube Buddy does not send it to a Cube Buddy account or server. Clearing the browser's site data removes it.

Photos and the cube scanner

If you choose to take or upload a photo, Cube Buddy reads it in your browser to identify cube sticker colors. The page code does not upload the selected image to a Cube Buddy server. You can also enter colors without using a photo.

Third-party services and hosting

Cube Buddy loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive information such as your IP address and browser details under its own privacy practices. The service that hosts this website may also process standard connection information, such as access logs. Those services are governed by their own policies.

Children and parents

Cube Buddy is designed for beginners, including children. A parent or guardian can help manage browser permissions and clear locally stored solve history through the browser's site-data settings. Do not submit personal information through a device or service unless you have permission to do so.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Cube Buddy changes. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. For privacy questions, use the contact details provided by the site operator or the place where you accessed Cube Buddy.
